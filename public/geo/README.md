# public/geo — choropleth boundaries

Generated by `scripts/build_geo_assets.py` (stdlib Python; `--cache DIR`
keeps the downloaded sources, `--offline` refuses to download). Every file is
derived from a public-domain or permissively licensed source named below; no
geometry was drawn by hand. `index.json` catalogs the four GeoJSON files
(`id`, `file`, `label`, `kind`, `keyProps`, `bytes`, `features`, `source`,
`license`, `retrieved`). Coordinates are WGS84 lon/lat rounded to 4 decimals
(~11 m). Retrieved 2026-10-16.

Properties: states `name`, `code` (USPS), `fips` (2-digit); counties `name`,
`state` (USPS), `fips` (5-digit); countries `name`, `iso_a2`, `iso_a3`
(Natural Earth's `-99` sentinel is resolved through the `_EH` columns; Kosovo
has `iso_a2` `XK` and no `iso_a3`, N. Cyprus and Somaliland have neither, so
those three carry `null`); zones `zone`, `counties` (count), `weather_zones`
(ERCOT codes), `county_fips`, `note`.

`ercot-zone-counties.json` is a provenance sidecar (not in the index): every
county placed in a zone with its ERCOT weather zone and the land-area shares
that decided it, plus the ERCOT counties that could not be placed.

Simplification is a pure-Python Douglas–Peucker run on *arcs* (ring segments
between junction vertices, i.e. where three polygons meet or a shared edge
begins). Each arc is simplified once and reused by every polygon that
contains it, so neighbours keep bit-identical shared edges, no slivers or
gaps open up, and the zone dissolve can remove shared county edges exactly.
Tolerances are planar degrees. A ring that would collapse below three
distinct vertices keeps its original (rounded) vertices instead ("ring
fallbacks" above; tiny islands).

## ERCOT zones: derivation chain

The zone names are exactly the `zone` values of
`data/datasets/ercot-system-hourly-2024.csv` (and the 2019–2025 files):
Houston, North, Northeast, Panhandle, South, South_Central, West. They are
the market-simulator model's seven ERCOT transmission zones, and the model
defines them two ways:

1. **Load side (the `demand_mw` column): a weather-zone crosswalk.**
   `market-simulator/src/market_sim/config/iso_configs.py:143-156` ("Load
   shares are derived from ERCOT NP6-345-CD actual load by weather zone by
   aggregating the 8 weather zones onto the 7 transmission zones … Panhandle
   carries no modeled load: ERCOT has no Panhandle weather zone") and
   `:166-168` ("Weather zone -> transmission zone: West <- FAR_WEST + WEST;
   North <- NORTH_C + NORTH; Northeast <- EAST; Houston <- COAST;
   South_Central <- SOUTH_C; South <- SOUTHERN"); the same table as code in
   `market-simulator/scripts/data/derive_load_shares.py:81-90`
   (`WZ_TO_ZONE`), and restated in
   `market-simulator/src/market_sim/config/constants.py:4340-4352` and
   `:4432-4442` (`DATACENTER_ZONE_SHARE["ERCOT"]`, "no ERCOT weather zone
   maps here -> 0" for Panhandle). `constants.py:6333-6338` adds that the
   Northeast zone "IS the EAST weather zone".
2. **Generation side: a lat/lon rule on plant coordinates.**
   `market-simulator/src/market_sim/data/zone_assignment.py:740-790`
   (`_ercot_zone`): Houston-metro counties first (`HOUSTON_COUNTIES`,
   `:224-238`), then `lon < -99.5` → Panhandle if `lat >= 33.5` else West
   (`:771-777`), a North band, a Northeast window, South_Central and South
   latitude bands.

Because the datasets' demand is the weather-zone aggregation, the six load
zones are built from ERCOT's weather zones; the Panhandle zone, which no
weather zone feeds, is built with the model's own geographic rule. The
county→zone assignment runs in four steps, all in
`scripts/build_geo_assets.py`:

**Step 1 — which Texas counties are in ERCOT.** The ERCOT County Map from the
ERCOT media kit
(https://www.ercot.com/files/assets/2022/12/13/ERCOT-Maps_Area-by-county.jpg,
linked from https://ercot.com/news/mediakit/maps) names every county and
shades the ones outside ERCOT grey. The 40 grey counties are transcribed
verbatim into `NON_ERCOT_TX_COUNTIES` (Panhandle/South Plains: Dallam,
Sherman, Hansford, Ochiltree, Lipscomb, Hartley, Moore, Hutchinson, Hemphill,
Bailey, Lamb, Cochran, Hockley, Yoakum, Terry, Gaines; far west: El Paso,
Hudspeth; east: Bowie, Cass, Morris, Marion, Harrison, Gregg, Upshur, Camp,
Panola, Shelby, San Augustine, Sabine; southeast: Trinity, Polk, San Jacinto,
Tyler, Jasper, Newton, Hardin, Liberty, Jefferson, Orange). The other 214
counties are ERCOT counties. (ERCOT's ZIP table in step 2 cannot decide
membership: it also maps non-ERCOT ZIPs such as Beaumont, Jasper and El
Paso to weather zones.)

**Step 2 — which ERCOT weather zone each county is in.** ERCOT's Load
Profiling Guide Appendix D, "Profile Decision Tree" workbook
(https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2024/04/30/Appendix_D_Profile_Decision_Tree_050124.xlsx,
sheet `ZipToZone`, "ZIP Code to Weather Zone Table", 2,476 ZIPs with codes
COAST, EAST, FWEST, NCENT, NORTH, SCENT, SOUTH, WEST) is joined to the US
Census 2020 ZCTA→county relationship file
(https://www2.census.gov/geo/docs/maps-data/data/rel2020/zcta520/tab20_zcta520_county20_natl.txt,
public domain). Every (ZCTA, county) part whose ZCTA is in ERCOT's table
votes with its land area (`AREALAND_PART`) for that ZIP's weather zone; the
county takes the zone with the largest area. 204 of the 210 placed counties
have a ≥ 80 % majority; the closest calls are Kent (NORTH 0.62 / WEST 0.38),
Lynn (FWEST 0.65 / NORTH 0.35), Austin (COAST 0.67 / SCENT 0.33),
Shackelford (NCENT 0.72), Burnet (SCENT 0.73) and Borden (FWEST 0.77); the
shares are in `ercot-zone-counties.json`. Four ERCOT counties have no ZIP in
ERCOT's table at all (Castro, Hale, Parmer, Randall — SPS-dominated counties
with small cooperative pockets) and are therefore **not placed in any zone**
rather than guessed; they are listed under `unassigned` in the sidecar.

**Step 3 — weather zone → model zone** with the crosswalk of item 1
(`WEATHER_ZONE_TO_MODEL_ZONE`): COAST→Houston, NCENT+NORTH→North,
EAST→Northeast, SCENT→South_Central, SOUTH→South, FWEST+WEST→West.

**Step 4 — Panhandle.** No ERCOT weather zone maps to the model's Panhandle
zone, so the model's own Panhandle rule (`zone_assignment.py:771-777`:
`lon < -99.5 and lat >= 33.5`) is evaluated on each placed county's centroid
(`carve_panhandle`); 24 counties move from North to Panhandle (Armstrong,
Briscoe, Carson, Childress, Collingsworth, Cottle, Crosby, Deaf Smith,
Dickens, Donley, Floyd, Foard, Gray, Hall, Hardeman, King, Knox, Lubbock,
Motley, Oldham, Potter, Roberts, Swisher, Wheeler). The Panhandle feature
carries `weather_zones: []` and a `note` saying so.

**Dissolve.** Each zone is the union of its member county polygons: outer
rings oriented counter-clockwise, holes clockwise, every undirected edge used
by two rings removed, the remaining directed edges chained into rings (left-
most turn at pinch vertices), rings with positive area as outer rings and
negative ones as holes. The dissolved area must equal the members' total
area to 1e-6 or the build fails, so the output is a true union, not a
MultiPolygon of county pieces. Houston and South are MultiPolygons (coastal
islands); the other five are single Polygons.

Result: 210 counties placed (West 52, North 50, South 26, Panhandle 24,
South_Central 24, Northeast 21, Houston 13), 4 unassigned, 40 outside ERCOT.

### Caveats

- Membership (step 1) is read from an official ERCOT image, not a text
  list; ERCOT publishes no county list. The transcription is a named,
  reviewable 40-entry constant that the build validates against the counties
  file (an unknown name fails the build).
- ERCOT's own weather-zone map (https://www.ercot.com/files/assets/2023/04/26/Weather.jpg)
  notes that it "does not necessarily include all counties that have
  participation within the ERCOT market"; the Amarillo-area ERCOT counties
  (Potter, Carson, Gray, Wheeler, Deaf Smith, Oldham, Randall, Armstrong,
  Donley, Collingsworth, Roberts) are shown outside that map although the
  County Map shows them as (partially) ERCOT. They land in Panhandle here.
- The Panhandle carve is the model's generation-side rule applied to county
  centroids; on the load side the model gives Panhandle zero demand, so the
  24 NORTH-weather-zone counties it takes from North (Lubbock pocket,
  Childress and Amarillo areas) contribute their demand to the dataset's
  North rows. The rule also captures Hardeman, Foard and Knox (Rolling
  Plains, lon < -99.5).
- `iso_configs.py:153-156` says the "small Lubbock load … is reported inside
  the West weather zone"; ERCOT's ZIP table puts every Lubbock ZIP in NORTH
  and the 2022 Long-Term Load Forecast report states "Lubbock's forecasted
  load was added to the North weather zone"
  (https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2022/02/24/2022_LTLF_Report.pdf). This
  file follows ERCOT.
- ZIP codes and Census ZCTAs are not identical; 643 of ERCOT's ZIPs (PO-box
  and unique ZIPs) have no ZCTA and cast no vote. Land area, not load,
  weighs the vote.
- ERCOT's weather zones are defined by ZIP, so a county split between two
  weather zones is placed whole in its majority zone (closest calls above).
